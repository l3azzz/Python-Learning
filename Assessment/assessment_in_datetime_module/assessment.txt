Question 16
q: datetime module ഉപയോഗിച്ച് Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow dates print ചെയ്യുക. (Using the datetime module, print the dates for Yesterday, Today, and Tomorrow.)
a: from datetime import date, timedelta
   
   today = date.today()
   yesterday = today - timedelta(days=1)
   tomorrow = today + timedelta(days=1)
   
   print("Yesterday:", yesterday)
   print("Today:", today)
   print("Tomorrow:", tomorrow)
